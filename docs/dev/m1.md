## Data accessibility: both are open, no request needed

**TAC (thermoacoustic combustor):**
Dryad, DOI `10.5061/dryad.4cj4k`, CC-BY, immediate download. Source paper: Bonciolini, Ebi, Boujo & Noiray, "Experiments and modelling of rate-dependent transition delay in a stochastic subcritical bifurcation," R. Soc. Open Sci. 5(3), 172078 (2018).

**DaphniaExt (Daphnia nutrition microcosm):**
Dryad, DOI `10.5061/dryad.q3p64`, published Jan 2015, also open. Source paper: Drake & Griffen, "Early warning signals of extinction in deteriorating environments," Nature 467, 456–459 (2010). The study used replicate laboratory populations of Daphnia magna, showing that populations crossing an experimentally induced transcritical bifurcation exhibited statistical signatures of critical slowing down starting as early as 110 days — roughly 8 generations — before the actual transition, while constant-environment control populations showed no such pattern.
